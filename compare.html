How to interpret the results

Five-year interest plus fees counts the first 60 scheduled months of mortgage interest plus entered upfront loan fees. It is not a measure of total homeownership cost, and it does not include property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA dues, mortgage insurance, rate changes, investment returns, or tax effects. The remaining principal is shown separately because it is debt still owed, not a fee.

When comparing offers with different loan amounts or terms, neither the five-year interest figure nor the monthly payment alone establishes which loan is best. Compare the same loan type and review the full lender-provided Loan Estimates, APR, cash to close, projected payments, and loan features. This tool models fully amortizing fixed-rate loans with monthly payments, no extra principal payments, and no prepayment penalties.
